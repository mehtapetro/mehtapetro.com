Rubber process oils play a crucial role in modern rubber compounding, improving processability, dispersion of fillers, and end-use properties. Mehta Petro's R&D team has identified optimized formulations and application approaches for aromatic and paraffinic process oils tailored for tire and industrial rubber compounds.

Key benefits observed:

- Improved mixing torque and dispersion leading to more uniform compounds.
- Enhanced low-temperature flexibility and reduced stiffness in finished rubber.
- Controlled viscosity modifications to balance processing and physical properties.

Mehta Petro is offering technical support and sample evaluation for compounders seeking to test optimized process oils in their formulations. Contact our technical team for detailed specs and trial arrangements.
